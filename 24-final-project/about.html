Taco Trivia

When did Tacos first appear in the United States

Jeffrey M.Pitcher, Taco historian and a professor of history at the University of Minnesota, says the earliest mention of Tacos in the United States are in a newspaper from 1905. (source: Smithsonian Magazine )
